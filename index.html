Bio

My name is Ashlyn (ASH-LEN) and I feel my happiest when I see people learn—whether it's a vocational skill, a mathematical equation, or a moment that introduces them to a new space of reflection. So naturally, I am a PhD student in the School of Information at the University of Michigan, mentored by Barbara Ericson and Mark Guzdial. I study the design of informal online learning communities to broaden participation in computing, particularly in the context of Computing Education, Human-Computer Interaction, and Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence. I earned my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Sociology, where my undergraduate research centered on near-peer mentorship models and educational applications of machine learning.
