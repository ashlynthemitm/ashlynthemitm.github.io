Bio

My name is Ashlyn (ASH-LEN), and I am a doctoral student in the School of Information at the University of Michigan advised by Dr. Patricia Garcia. My research falls at the intersection of AI in education and Human-Centered Design. My primary interest is centering student knowledge, experience as users, and agency in research on generative AI in computing. My research is supported by both the Rackham Merit Fellowship (RMF) and the National Science Foundation (NSF) Graduate Research Fellowship. I have a Bachelor's in Computer Science, a minor in Sociology, and a Data Science Certification from Georgia State University (GSU). During my time at GSU, Dr. Anu Bourgeois and I co-founded the RISE in Computing Stars, a research-informed intervention for freshmen and sophomore college students in computing. Outside of my research, I am passionate about academic & professional mentorship, STEM outreach, and public scholarship. In my free time, I enjoy writing whatever comes to mind on my Substack and drawing!
