Reaching for Inclusion, Striving for Excellence (RISE) in Computing Stars, commonly referred to as RISE Stars, is a targeted initative and intervention aimed at broadening participation in the field of computing by supporting the academic and professional success of Black women in computer science at Georgia State University. This program unites faculty, upperclass students, and freshmen and sophomore students to tackle the systemic challenges faced by Black women in the tech industry.
RISE Stars is founded on four essential pillars: near-peer mentorship, collaborative projects, community building, and professional development. These components promote a sense of belonging and encourage persistence within the field.
