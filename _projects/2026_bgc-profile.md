As a co-instructor for the [2026 Black Girls Code Detroit Summer Camp](https://www.wearebgc.org/summer-camp), I helped teach game design to elementary and middle school students ranging from ages 10-15 through hands-on, collaborative activities. I worked alongside fellow instructors at [Code 313](https://www.code313detroit.org/) in Detroit to guide campers as they explored the fundamentals of game development, from designing sprites to building their own platformer games. Beyond technical instruction, I mentored students, encouraged creative problem-solving, and supported an identity-affirming learning environment where every student could build confidence in their coding and design abilities. The campers created fantastic projects and I learned a lot about teaching children and teens how to code!
